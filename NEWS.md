# hibiiscus 0.1

## New features

* Create, update, and filter PREDICTS-like databases; add external site, species, and site-contrast data.
* Define custom land-use legends and correct abundance measurements for sampling effort.
* Calculate within-site diversity, between-site dissimilarity, and land-use, geographic, and environmental site contrasts.
* Fit mixed-effects models with `lme4`, `brms`, or INLA, including weighted analyses across multiple datasets.
* Evaluate models with random or grouped cross-validation, including predictive performance and fold-level coefficients.
* Project models to tabular or raster covariates, estimate prediction uncertainty, and calculate the Area of Applicability.
* Prepare downscaled GLOBIOM NetCDF outputs for projection, and plot database, model, cross-validation, and projection results.
* Save and reload hibiiscus database, land-use legend, model, cross-validation, and projection objects.
